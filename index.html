Fruity Blast privacy policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

In short: Fruity Blast does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads, as explained below.

Who we are

Fruity Blast (the “app”) is a free puzzle game for Android made by Nazo (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to us at nazoxyz022@gmail.com.

Information we store on your device

The app saves your game progress, such as the level you have reached and your score, on your device. This information is not sent to us and is not shared with anyone. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

Ads and information collected by Google AdMob

Fruity Blast is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may automatically collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with ads and the app, such as ad views and taps
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ads perform

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how ads perform, detect fraud and keep its services secure. We do not receive your personal information from Google. To learn more, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Some ads in the app are optional rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch one to get extra time or a free booster.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (usually under Settings, then Google, then Ads).
  • Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to remove the game data stored on your device.

Children

Fruity Blast is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only information that leaves your device because of the app is the ad-related data that the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google, and that data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Please check this page now and then.
